About RuntimeLoop

Building across ideas, not categories.

RuntimeLoop is an independent technology-focused app studio creating practical tools, playful experiences, and innovative digital products. We explore different ideas across software, entertainment, learning, productivity, and emerging technology.

Curious by nature.
Useful by design.

From small utilities to interactive games and experimental technology, every RuntimeLoop project starts with a simple question: can we build something people enjoy using? We keep the experience focused, thoughtful, and easy to use.

RuntimeLoop is a one-person studio. We build education apps, simple games and everyday utilities, and every release follows the same three rules.

Collect only what is needed
If an app does not need your data, it does not ask for it. Hatekhori collects none at all.
Respect attention
No dark patterns, no pressure to keep tapping, and no ads shown to children.
Release, listen, improve
We ship small, read every review and message, and make each version better than the last.
